# 01 — Demo Objective

## The business thesis
Manufacturers often already possess product data, CAD/BIM files, and traditional faceted catalogs. The missing layer is frequently **decision support**: helping a buyer/specifier determine which product or configuration fits the job before they reach the technical file library.

This demo proves the value of that layer without waiting for system access.

## What we are proving
- Natural language can become structured product requirements.
- Product matching can remain deterministic and auditable.
- A complex model family can be navigated through use-case questions instead of catalog knowledge.
- Product dimensions can drive a meaningful visual and fit calculation.
- The result can hand off to existing technical resources rather than replacing them.

## What we are not proving yet
- Live pricing
- Inventory/availability
- Dealer routing
- Quote generation
- Full catalog coverage
- Engineering-grade CAD generation
- Manufacturer-approved configuration rules
- ERP/PIM/KCL integration

## Demo audience
John Boos leadership, digital/ecommerce, sales, product, specification, dealer/channel, or PE/portfolio stakeholders.

## Success signal
A viewer should immediately understand:

> “Instead of expecting a customer to know our model numbers, we can ask what they are building and guide them to the right product.”
